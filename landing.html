    Sketch with the pen you already use.

    Draw the free-body diagram on your tablet and it appears on the PC as you draw. Pan on one screen and the other follows. The eraser takes out only the part of a stroke it touches.

    Select a sketch and its equations and ask Claude to turn them into a live widget: a draggable beam, a moment diagram that follows your sliders. Or ask it to review every equation for sign errors, missing integration constants and units, with a note beside each fix.
